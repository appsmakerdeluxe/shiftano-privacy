Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a shift rotation pattern?

Navigate to "Patterns" from the menu. Define your sequence (e.g. Day-Day-Night-Night-Off-Off) and roll it out over any selected date range with one tap.

How does the roster scanner work?

Tap "Scan Roster" and choose an image or PDF of your shift schedule. Shiftano's on-device OCR extracts dates and shift codes, allowing you to preview and approve before applying changes.
