Privacy Policy for Shiftano & Shiftano Pro

Summary: Shiftano and Shiftano Pro store all your rosters, shift patterns, notes, salary rates, and time records strictly locally on your device. There are no user accounts, no analytics tracking, no advertisements, and no cloud servers.

1. Data Controller

Responsible developer:
AppsMakerDeluxe Studios
Email: appsmakerdeluxe@gmail.com

2. Core Privacy Philosophy

Shiftano and Shiftano Pro are designed with Privacy by Default. Both editions function completely offline without requiring any user account or online registration.

3. Data Stored Locally

  • Roster Data: Shift types, start and end times, notes, work locations, rotation patterns.
  • Time Tracking Data: Clock-in and clock-out timestamps, break durations, actual work hours.
  • Profiles & Contracts: Target hours, overtime balances, vacation allowances, hourly wage rates, and bonus rules (used strictly for local calculation in Shiftano Pro).
  • Shift Swaps (Pro): Swap partners, traded shifts, and approval status (documented locally only).

All of this data is stored exclusively in your device's local SQLite/Room database.

4. Permissions Used

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely if you opt to enable shift reminders or alarms.
  • Camera / Photo Picker: Used exclusively on-device when you choose to photograph or import a paper roster for text recognition (OCR via on-device ML Kit). No image data leaves your phone.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Required to trigger timely reminders before your shifts start.

5. Backups and File Exports

When you generate a local backup or export records as a PDF timesheet, ICS calendar file, or CSV spreadsheet, the file is saved via Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF) or shared via the standard Android Sharesheet. No background upload to third-party servers takes place.

6. No Third-Party Sharing

Because we do not operate remote servers and do not integrate any advertising or analytics SDKs, your data is never transmitted, processed, or sold to anyone.

7. User Control & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times. You can delete individual shifts, reset the database in the app settings, or uninstall the app to permanently and irreversibly erase all stored information from your device.

8. Contact

If you have any questions regarding privacy, please contact: appsmakerdeluxe@gmail.com
